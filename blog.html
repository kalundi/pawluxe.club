A safer summer walking routine

Plan walks around cooler hours, test pavement with the back of your hand, bring water, and watch for slowing, heavy panting, or unusual behavior.

Shorter enrichment walks can be just as rewarding as long mileage. Let your dog sniff, choose a shaded route, and save energetic games for indoors when temperatures climb.

Enrichment · 4 min read

Five easy boredom busters

Rotate familiar toys, scatter part of a meal for a sniffing game, practice two-minute training sessions, freeze a pet-safe food puzzle, or create a supervised cardboard search box.

Introduce one activity at a time and match the difficulty to your pet. The goal is calm curiosity—not frustration.

Prepare your pet for a sitter
